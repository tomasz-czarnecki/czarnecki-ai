05Deep dive

Buildings that were programs.

Five years in China. I wrote the scripts behind a facade and an opera house interior at MAD, and competition wins at AREP. A decade before "AI agent" was a phrase, I was building evolutionary solvers that bred building geometry from a fitness function.

MAD Architects · Xiamen, 2015–2016

Xinhee Design Center

Skin and bones

A design center for the fashion group Xinhee: six office arms around a central atrium, wrapped in a floating PTFE skin. I wrote the genetic algorithms that bred that skin, balancing structural load against construction cost.

My part: I built the parametric model that generated the double-curved membrane facade and made it possible to build. There was no precedent to copy, so the model optimised for two things at once: membrane cost and buildability.

MAD Architects · Harbin, 2015

Harbin Opera House

A building carved by snow and wind

The focal point of Harbin's Cultural Island on the Songhua River, won by MAD in an international competition in 2010 and completed in 2015. A sinuous white shell over a 1,600-seat grand theater and a 400-seat small theater.

My part: the parametric design of the timber interior elements, detailed all the way to fabrication so the site could build them straight from the model.

08About

The model isn't the hard part. It's the human layer: the trust, adoption, and judgment calls that decide whether people actually act on it.

My specialty is uncertainty UX: turning model output and confidence into explainable, simulatable interfaces that non-technical teams trust and act on. On every product I've shipped, the bottleneck was never model accuracy. It was adoption.

Case study · MAD Architects, 2016

A building with skin and bones

Xinhee Design Center is a 61,000 m² headquarters for a fashion group in Xiamen: a solid "bone" structure of six arms around a central atrium, wrapped in a translucent PTFE skin.

As the algorithmic designer, I built the model and made sure the facade could actually be built. I wrote the Grasshopper program that drove Galapagos, an evolutionary solver that bred thousands of facade variants against structural load and cost until the geometry was constructible. The building was a program.
